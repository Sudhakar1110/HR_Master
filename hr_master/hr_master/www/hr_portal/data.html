Data Protection Policy

Last updated: {{ frappe.utils.today() }}

1. Purpose

This policy describes how {{ company_name }} protects the personal data of job applicants and candidates processed through this Recruitment Portal.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all personal data of candidates and users collected, stored or processed through the portal, including resumes, contact details, employment history, skills and assessment results.

3. Data protection principles

  • Lawfulness & fairness: data is processed only for legitimate recruitment purposes.
  • Purpose limitation: data is used only for the stated recruitment purposes.
  • Data minimisation: only data necessary for evaluation is collected.
  • Accuracy: reasonable steps are taken to keep data accurate and up to date.
  • Storage limitation: data is retained no longer than necessary.
  • Integrity & confidentiality: data is protected with appropriate technical and organisational measures.

4. Access control

Access to candidate data is granted only to authorised HR staff based on role. All access is authenticated, and significant actions are recorded in an audit log.

5. Consent & transparency

Where required by law, candidates are informed about how their data is used and may be asked for consent. Automated evaluation (match scoring) assists, but does not replace, human decision-making.

6. Data subject rights

Candidates may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection or portability of their data. Requests are handled by the HR department within applicable time limits.

7. Breach handling

Suspected data breaches are reported immediately to the designated data protection contact and handled in line with applicable law, including notification obligations.

8. Training & accountability

HR staff using this portal receive appropriate guidance on data protection. The organisation maintains records of processing activities and reviews this policy periodically.

9. Contact

For data protection enquiries, contact our HR department.
