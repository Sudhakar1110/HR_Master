Cookie Policy

Last updated: {{ frappe.utils.today() }}

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. This portal is built on the Frappe/ERPNext platform, which uses cookies for essential functions.

2. Cookies we use

  • Essential/session cookies: required to keep you logged in and maintain a secure session (e.g. sid). These cannot be disabled.
  • Security cookies: used to prevent cross-site request forgery (CSRF) attacks (e.g. csrf_token).
  • Preferences: the platform may store user interface preferences locally.

3. Managing cookies

Essential cookies are necessary for the portal to function. You can clear cookies through your browser settings at any time, but doing so may log you out or affect functionality.

4. Third-party cookies

When portal features integrate with third-party services (e.g. job portal APIs), those services may set their own cookies or tokens in accordance with their own policies.

5. Contact

Questions about this Cookie Policy should be directed to our HR department.
